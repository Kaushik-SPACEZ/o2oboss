# O2O Boss - User Roles & Responsibilities

## 👥 Six User Types in O2O Boss

---

## 1. 👤 CUSTOMER
**"One Stop Solutions FOR ALL"**

### Who They Are
Regular users seeking quality products/services from QPS RIGHTS™ certified vendors.

### Journey
1. Browse Products/Services → 2. Select Vendor → 3. Place Order → 4. Pay → 5. Receive → 6. Review

### Value: Trust, quality assurance, competitive pricing, single platform for all needs.

---

## 2. 🏪 VENDOR
**"QPS RIGHTS™ Certified Quality"**

### Who They Are
Local businesses, shop owners, service providers wanting wider customer reach.

### Journey
1. Apply for Certification → 2. Get Verified → 3. List Products → 4. Receive Orders → 5. Fulfill → 6. Get Paid

### Value: Customer access, trust badge, marketing support, back office help.

---

## 3. 💼 SALES PARTNER
**"THE SKY IS NOT THE LIMIT..."**

### Who They Are
Individuals earning by referring customers, vendors, and partners. Part-time or full-time.

### Journey
1. Join → 2. Get Training → 3. Refer Customers → 4. Refer Vendors → 5. Build Team → 6. Earn Commissions

### Earnings
- Customer referral: Commission on purchases
- Vendor referral: Onboarding bonus + ongoing %
- Partner referral: Team building bonus

---

## 4. 🏢 FRANCHISE PARTNER
**"Upholding Entrepreneurial Spirit"**

### Who They Are
Entrepreneurs owning O2O Boss franchise in their territory (Coffee Kiosks, Ayurvedic Spas, etc.)

### Franchise Types
- ☕ Coffee Kiosk
- 🧘 Ayurvedic Spa Center
- 🏪 O2O Boss Retail Outlet
- 📦 Distribution Center

### Journey
1. Apply → 2. Territory Selection → 3. Pay Fee → 4. Receive Setup → 5. Get Training → 6. Operate → 7. Pay Royalty

### O2O Boss Provides: Complete setup, branding, inventory, training, ongoing support.

---

## 5. 🎧 BACK OFFICE
**"I am the reason for everything."**

### Who They Are
Internal team handling operations and supporting all stakeholders.

### Functions
- Customer Support
- Vendor Verification
- Order Management
- Payment Processing
- Partner Training
- Issue Resolution

---

## 6. ⚙️ ADMIN
**"Be an instrument of Socio-Economic Change"**

### Who They Are
Leadership overseeing the entire ecosystem.

### Functions
- Strategic Planning
- User Management
- Franchise Approvals
- Business Analytics
- Quality Control (QPS RIGHTS™)
- Platform Security

---

## 🔄 How Roles Connect

```
ADMIN → oversees → BACK OFFICE + FRANCHISE + VENDOR
BACK OFFICE → supports → ALL USERS
SALES PARTNERS → refer → CUSTOMERS + VENDORS + MORE PARTNERS
FRANCHISE → operates → TERRITORY BUSINESS
VENDORS → sell to → CUSTOMERS
```
