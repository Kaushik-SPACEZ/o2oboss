# O2O Boss - Vendor & Sales Partner Flow

## 🏪 VENDOR FLOW

### Who is a Vendor?
Local businesses, shop owners, service providers who join O2O Boss to reach more customers.

---

### Vendor Onboarding Journey

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                  VENDOR ONBOARDING                          │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

1. APPLY
   └── Submit business details, documents, photos
           ↓
2. VERIFICATION
   └── Back office reviews application
   └── Physical verification if needed
           ↓
3. QPS RIGHTS™ CERTIFICATION
   └── Quality check passed
   └── Receive certification badge
           ↓
4. ONBOARDING
   └── Profile setup
   └── Product/service listing
   └── Pricing configuration
           ↓
5. TRAINING
   └── App usage training
   └── Order management training
           ↓
6. GO LIVE
   └── Start receiving orders!
```

---

### Vendor Daily Operations

```
┌──────────────────────────────────────┐
│      VENDOR DAILY CYCLE             │
└──────────────────────────────────────┘

  Morning: Check new orders
      ↓
  Accept/Prepare orders
      ↓
  Fulfill delivery/service
      ↓
  Update order status
      ↓
  Evening: Check earnings
      ↓
  Weekly: Receive payouts
```

---

### Vendor Earnings Model

| Component | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| Sale Price | What customer pays |
| Platform Commission | O2O Boss % (varies by category) |
| Net Earning | Sale Price - Commission |
| Payout | Weekly/bi-weekly settlement |

## 💼 SALES PARTNER FLOW

### Who is a Sales Partner?
Individuals who earn by referring customers, vendors, and other partners to O2O Boss.

**"THE SKY IS NOT THE LIMIT... Earn How Much Ever you want @ anytime from anywhere"**

---

### Sales Partner Journey

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│              SALES PARTNER LIFECYCLE                        │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

1. JOIN
   └── Sign up as Sales Partner
   └── Complete KYC
           ↓
2. TRAINING
   └── Understand O2O Boss model
   └── Learn referral techniques
   └── Get marketing materials
           ↓
3. REFER CUSTOMERS
   └── Share referral link/code
   └── Earn on their purchases
           ↓
4. REFER VENDORS
   └── Identify local businesses
   └── Help them onboard
   └── Earn onboarding bonus
           ↓
5. BUILD TEAM
   └── Refer new sales partners
   └── Earn from team performance
           ↓
6. GROW & EARN
   └── More referrals = More earnings
   └── No cap on income!
```

---

### Sales Partner Earning Streams

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│              THREE WAYS TO EARN                             │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

1. CUSTOMER REFERRALS
   └── Get % commission when referred customers buy
   └── Ongoing passive income

2. VENDOR REFERRALS  
   └── One-time onboarding bonus
   └── Ongoing % of vendor's sales

3. PARTNER REFERRALS
   └── Bonus when new partner joins
   └── % of their earnings (MLM structure)
```

---

### Commission Structure Example

| Referral Type | Immediate | Ongoing |
|---------------|-----------|---------|
| Customer | - | 2-5% of purchases |
| Vendor | ₹500-2000 bonus | 0.5-1% of sales |
| Sales Partner | ₹200-500 bonus | 10-20% of their commission |

*Actual rates may vary*
