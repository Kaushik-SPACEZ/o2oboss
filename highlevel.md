Just FYI 
Here is the *O2OBOSS Service Booking + Quotation — Full Screen Flow*. Give this to your designer/dev, they can build it directly.

BUYER FLOW (Customer)

*1. Home*
- Search bar: "Product or Service?"
- Two tabs:
- Services categories: AC Repair, Plumbing, Electrical, Cleaning, Painting, Civil Work etc[Products][Services]

*2. Service List*
- Filter: Near Me, Rating, Price, Available Today
- Each card: Vendor Photo, Name, Rating (4.8), Jobs Done (230), Distance (2.3km), Starting Price

*3. Vendor Profile*
- Photos of work, Reviews, Services offered, Service area map
- Buttons: [Request Site Visit][Call][Chat]

*4. Request Site Visit Form*
- What is the issue? (text + upload 3 photos/video)
- Address (auto GPS)
- Select Date & Time Slot
- Button: [Confirm Visit - Free / AED 20]

*5. Quotation Received Screen*
- You get 3 quotes in 1 screen to compare (like PolicyBazaar)
- Each quote: Price breakdown, Material cost, Labour, Timeline, Warranty
- Vendor note: "Needs compressor change"
- Buttons:[Accept][Negotiate][Reject]

*6. Negotiation Chat*
- In-app chat + Price counter offer
- System message: "Vendor reduced to AED 450 from 500"
- Button: [Pay Advance 20% to Confirm]

*7. Job Tracking*
- Status: Vendor on the way (Live GPS) > OTP Start > Work in Progress > Completed
- Upload final photos by vendor
- Pay Remaining + Give Review

PARTNER FLOW (Service Vendor App)

*1. Lead Inbox*
- New Request: "AC not cooling - 2.1km away - Photos attached"
- Buttons: [Accept Lead][Decline]

*2. Site Visit*
- Navigate to customer (Google Maps)
- Start Visit with OTP
- Checklist + Take site photos
- Button: [Create Quotation]

*3. Create Quotation*
- Add Items: Labour: AED 200, Compressor: AED 350
- Add Warranty: 6 months
- Add Validity: 3 days
- Send Quote

*4. Earnings*
- Jobs Won, Pending Payment, Commission Deducted

ADMIN FLOW (You)

- See all site visit requests
- Auto-assign vendor if not accepted in 30 mins
- Commission auto-cut after customer pays
- Dispute button: If customer says work not done

*Critical Logic to Add:*
- If vendor takes customer offline (shares phone number), block chat — detects numbers.
- Advance payment must go through DSG wallet, or you lose money.

This flow keeps your app at *42MB* even with services.
